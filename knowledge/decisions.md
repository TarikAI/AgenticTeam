# Reusable Decisions

Cross-build decision patterns promoted from per-build `.agentic-team/runs/<run-id>/DECISIONS.md` files.
Only promote decisions likely to recur (stack combos, provider choices, architecture
patterns). Include the conditions under which the decision holds.
