# Council artifacts

- `brief.md`: frozen question, facts, unknowns, constraints, rubric, owner.
- `proposals/<agent>.md`: thesis, design, assumptions, evidence, risks, costs, validation.
- `critiques/<agent>.md`: target claims, strongest objection, failure mode, required evidence.
- `synthesis.md`: coherent chosen design, interfaces, integrated elements, confidence.
- `dissent.md`: unresolved minority findings, why rejected, consequence if correct.
- `decision.md`: outcome, rationale, owner, next tasks, experiment and reopening triggers.

The moderator may request missing evidence but may not rewrite a contributor's original record.
