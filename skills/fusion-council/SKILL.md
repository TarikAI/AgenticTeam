# Fusion Council

Read `references/council-artifacts.md`. Use the deterministic fusion workspace rather than a
single conversational role-play.

## Run the council

1. Freeze the decision brief: question, constraints, facts, unknowns, success rubric, decision
   owner, autonomy policy, and deadline.
2. Choose a moderator, a verifier, and at least two contributors with genuinely different
   specialties. Add challengers for high-risk dimensions.
3. Collect first proposals independently. Do not reveal or summarize one proposal to another.
4. Reveal together, then assign cross-critiques against the frozen rubric.
5. Allow one bounded revision when critique introduces evidence.
6. Moderator selects a coherent spine and writes synthesis; incompatible foundations are not
   averaged. Preserve material rejected arguments in the dissent ledger.
7. Verifier checks requirements, evidence, internal consistency, risks, missing perspectives,
   and executable next tasks.
8. Accept automatically only if policy allows; otherwise open a human checkpoint.

## Quality bar

The final decision must name assumptions, confidence, chosen/rejected elements, interfaces,
experiments, owner, reopening triggers, and next tasks. Vote count is never a substitute for
evidence or coherence.
